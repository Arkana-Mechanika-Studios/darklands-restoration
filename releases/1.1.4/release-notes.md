Darklands Restoration 1.1.4 is a small fix to 1.1 for players who use the HD fonts.

## Fixed

- **The pointer over HD text.** With the HD fonts (or any font pack with TrueType fonts), the gauntlet moved over a card's
  text or a store's lines showed under the letters, and the letters right next to it went missing while it was there. The
  pointer is now drawn over the text, and the letters it covers in part are drawn beside it. Wrong since 1.1.0, when font
  packs came.
- **Text of another screen over a card.** Coming back to a card from a store (the market's merchants, for example) or
  another screen, faint pieces of that screen's lines showed over the card's picture, and some letters of the card's own
  text had gaps. The card's window now takes back its picture whole. Wrong since 1.1.0.

Without a font pack the game is exactly as before: these fixes change nothing on the screen then.

Thanks to the player on Discord who reported both, with screenshots.
