Sound packs go here: one folder per pack, as you unzipped it, for example

  packs/audio/my-sound-pack/audio.json

Then open the launcher (DarklandsRP Launcher), its Advanced tab, the "Audio pack" row, and choose the pack with the arrows. A
sound pack replaces music and battle sounds; the game plays the same. More: docs/PACKS.md.

The Darklands Workshop (a download of its own) comes with a small example of generated test tones, in
Workshop/examples/example-audio-pack: copy that folder here to try it.

Unpacking a new version of the game over this one leaves this folder alone. Deleting the game's folder deletes it: keep a
copy of the packs you made.
