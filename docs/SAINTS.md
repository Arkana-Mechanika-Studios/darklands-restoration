# The saints in the Fixed rules

The saints whose help the Fixed rules change (the default for new games; Restored too), with everything each one gives. In
**Original** every saint helps as in the 1992 game. Each change has its entry in [FIXED.md](FIXED.md) ("Potions and saints").

A raise of a skill or an attribute lasts about a day (19 to 21 hours), then it is taken back. A heal is for good. Unless the
list says otherwise, a gift goes to the member prayed for, or to the leader when the prayer is for the whole party. The
saint's description in the game does not name every gift; those it leaves out are marked *(not in the description)*.

| Saint | What the 1992 game gives (Original) | What the Fixed rules give |
|---|---|---|
| **St. Anthony** | Endurance +4..9 and Strength +5..10 for a day, even above the maximum (taken back afterwards, so a member can end lower than he was); Charisma +1..10; Speak Common and Latin +10..19; Read/Write +20..39 | Endurance healed by 4..9 and Strength by 5..10, up to the maximum, for good; the rest as in Original |
| **St. Charity** | Standing +10..20 here *(not in the description)*; Flail weapons +15..29 *(not in the description)*; Charisma +6..11 *(not in the description)*; +32 armour against every blow, for good (instead of her fire protection) | Fire protection 90 for the member prayed for and 50 for the others, about a day; the rest as in Original. The healing of Endurance her description mentions is given by neither |
| **St. Christopher** | Streetwise +10..19, Woodwise +20..39, Riding +25..74, Bows +6..11 | The same, and Strength healed by a fifth of what is lost and Endurance by three tenths, and for a week the party travels overland half again as fast (encounters come per step, as before) |
| **St. Dismas** | Woodwise +10..19, Streetwise +20..39, Artifice +20..39, Agility +10..19, Stealth +10..19, all to the member prayed for | The member prayed for: the same, and Charisma +6..11 and Speak Common +6..11; every other member: Woodwise, Agility and Stealth +10..19 each |
| **St. Dunstan** | The skills of the weapons in hand +6..11; Perception +6..11; Artifice +25..74; Streetwise +6..11; Alchemy +5..14; +1 armour against every blow, for good | The same skills; instead of the armour, the party's metal armour (scale, chainmail, brigandine, plate) 20 points better for about a day |
| **St. Erasmus** | Agility +6..11; Perception +6..11; +32 armour against every blow, for good (instead of his fire protection) | The same skills; fire protection 50 for the party, about a day |
| **St. Michael** | The skills of the weapons in hand +20; Healing +20..39; Charisma +12..19; standing +20..40 here (in the open country, at the nearest city) | The same, and every member's weapons and armour 20 points better in a fight, about a day (up to 99) |
| **St. Pantaleon** | Endurance and Strength healed by three tenths of what is lost; Alchemy +10..19; Healing +15..29; Charisma +6..11 *(not in the description)*; the party may cross deep water *(not in the description)*; +32 armour against every blow, for good (instead of his help against fire and animals) | The same, and instead of the armour: fire protection 30 for the party and +1 armour against wolves, boars, bears and spiders, about a day |
| **St. Patrick** | Edged, Impact and Pole weapons, Speak Common, Latin and Read/Write +15..29; Charisma +15..24; Strength +12..19; +2 armour against every blow, for good | The same, and instead of the lasting armour: +2 armour against missiles for the member prayed for, about a day |
| **St. Polycarp** | Fire protection 90 for the party, and +48 armour against every blow, for good | Fire protection 90 for the party, about a day; no armour |
| **St. Thomas Aquinas** | Speak Common +12..26 *(not in the description)*; Latin +12..26; Read/Write +15..29; Healing +10..19 *(not in the description)*; Intelligence +12..23 | The same, and Religion +20..39; and where the city (in the open country, the nearest one) has a monastery or a university, standing +10..20 there |

Fire protection counts against flame weapons only (Arabian Fire, a dragon's breath). The armour "against every blow" of the
1992 game is a charm that never wears off; potions and charms of that kind already in a saved game stay as they were.

Every other saint helps as in the 1992 game, in every rule set. A review of all the saints' gifts and their balance is planned
before the saints' Fixed rules are final.
