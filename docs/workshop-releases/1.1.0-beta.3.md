The third beta of the Darklands Workshop, with the game's 1.1.4. Packs it saves record its version (`pack.json`,
`"workshop"`).

What changed since beta.2:

- **More texts to translate.** Texts the game drew without asking the pack are now in the template, and the editor lists
  them where they are shown:
  - the map's messages (`map:<n>.text`, DARKLAND.MSG), with the box of the map's message box;
  - the store's message when it is full (`id:rw.store.no-room`);
  - the battle menu's "(Ldr)" and "(All)";
  - the shop names the cards open barter with, and the guild master's "rebel" and "conservative";
  - the character generator's families and occupations;
  - the names of the dragons, the dwarfs and the titles the cards put into their texts.
- **A formula's short name has its room:** 61 px, the alchemy screen's batch list, though no recorded session showed it.
  The bar and the check measure it against that room.
- **Pronouns:** a pack's own `var-he`, `var-his` … (Others) now answer the game's pronouns in every card text.
- **The preview's "more"** at the foot of a long card text's pages is the pack's translation of it, no longer the English.
- **Play in game** shows the new texts with the game's 1.1.4 or later; an older game shows the original English there.

This is a beta: please report what goes wrong (the … menu, **Report a problem…**: a zip to attach to the report form;
nothing is sent).
