The second beta of the Darklands Workshop, with the game's 1.1.3. Packs it saves record its version (`pack.json`,
`"workshop"`).

What changed since beta.1:

- **The descriptions of the data files** are texts of their own: a saint's description (the prayer screen), a formula's
  (the alchemy screen's book and the formula page) and a city's (the card texts that show `$PlaceDesc`), under Saint
  prayer, Alchemy and the cards. About 300 texts, in `tables.ftl`.
- **Their room:** a description is drawn in its screen's column against the column's foot, and the bar says whether it
  fits ("Fits its box on the prayer screen · 9 px to spare") or how far it runs past. The check and the exports give the
  box as lines of a width ("12 lines of 156 px (font 2) on the prayer screen").
- **Play in game** shows them with the game's 1.1.3 or later; an older game shows the original English there.

Descriptions written by hand into a pack's `tables.ftl` for beta.1 are found and kept.

This is a beta: please report what goes wrong (the … menu, **Report a problem…**: a zip to attach to the report form;
nothing is sent).
