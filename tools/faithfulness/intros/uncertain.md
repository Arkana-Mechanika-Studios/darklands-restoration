Readings of the original that are guesses: the name or meaning of a field, a draft C that was
not checked against the disassembly, a mapping that could be wrong. Collected from the
`// UNCERTAIN:` comments (also those inside another comment). Each is a candidate for a check
against the original bytes (an oracle case or a look at the asm). `uncertain.csv` holds the
same data; the dormant branches (`// DORMANT:`, kept but unreachable) are listed in
`dormant.csv` only.
