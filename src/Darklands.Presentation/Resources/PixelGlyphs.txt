# The Darklands Restoration Project's own pixel glyphs (our drawings, not the game's): the Greek and Cyrillic letters that have no
# Latin look-alike in the game's fonts, and a few Latin letters the fonts lack. Used by the font-pack maker (PixelFontBuilder) to
# draw them in each of the game's three fonts; every other glyph of a made pack is taken from the player's own FONTS.FNT.
#
# "@ <letter> lower": 6 rows: the ascender row, the four rows of the x-height, the descender row.
# "@ <letter> upper": 7 rows: the six rows of a capital, then the descender row.
# '#' a pixel, '.' none; every row of a glyph has its width.
