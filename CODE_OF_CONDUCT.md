# Code of conduct

The Darklands Restoration Project is made by people who love an old game and want to share it. These few rules
keep it a good place to work, on GitHub, on the Discord and anywhere else the project meets.

## What we ask

* **Be kind and patient.** People come with different skills, languages and amounts of time. A question is never
  stupid, and English is not everyone's first language.
* **Talk about the work, not the person.** Disagree with an idea, a change or a report as much as you need to; never
  attack or mock the one who made it.
* **Assume good faith.** Most mistakes are honest. Point them out plainly and help fix them.
* **Give credit.** Say where your knowledge comes from, and name the people whose work you build on.
* **Respect the game's owners and the community's archives.** Never share the original game's files, or anything
  copied from them, through the project.

## What is not accepted

Harassment, insults, slurs or threats; sexual or violent content aimed at someone; personal attacks; publishing
someone's private information; and repeated disruption after being asked to stop.

## If something goes wrong

Write to the maintainers at arkana.mechanika.studios@gmail.com, or send a private message to a moderator on the
Discord. Reports are read in confidence. The maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or ask someone to
leave the project's spaces, for a time or for good, depending on what happened.
